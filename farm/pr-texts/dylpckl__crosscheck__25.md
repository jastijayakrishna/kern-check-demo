# Set the title in Kay Pho Du

## Summary

The app bar title now asks for Kay Pho Du from Google Fonts. Georgia stays behind it in the stack, so if the face carries no Latin glyphs or the request fails, the title renders in the serif it used before rather than in tofu. It requests weight 400, the one weight the family ships, so the browser has no reason to synthesise a heavier one.

The service worker caches the stylesheet stale-while-revalidate and the font files cache-first for a year, so an installed app keeps its title face offline.

## What I could not verify

The sandbox blocks `fonts.googleapis.com`, so I have confirmed the markup, the CSS, and the fallback path, but **not how the font actually looks, or whether Kay Pho Du includes Latin glyphs at all**. It's an SIL face designed for the Kayah Li script; most SIL fonts do ship Latin, but I couldn't check this one. If the title looks unchanged on your phone, that's the fallback doing its job and the family has no Latin coverage — tell me and I'll pick something else.
