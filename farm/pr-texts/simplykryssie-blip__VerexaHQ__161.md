# Staff setup checklist: popup instead of inline banner

## Summary

The staff onboarding/setup checklist previously sat as a permanent inline card at the top of the Dashboard, pushing content down until an admin dismissed it permanently. Converted it to a real popup, reusing the existing `Modal` component.

- Closing the popup (Cancel/Escape/backdrop click) is local to that page load -- reopens next dashboard visit, exactly matching today's visibility behavior. This is new: previously a non-admin staff member (who can't see the permanent-dismiss control) had no way to get it off their screen at all.
- The existing permanent "don't show again" action (admin-only, writes `workspaces.onboarding_dismissed_at`) is unchanged in behavior, just moved into the popup's footer as a text link.
- The nested "what does this step do?" explainer popup is unchanged (still a `Modal`, now stacking on top of the checklist modal).
