### [`v9.6.0`](https://redirect.github.com/cssnano/cssnano/releases/tag/cssnano%409.6.0): v9.6.0

[Compare Source](https://redirect.github.com/cssnano/cssnano/compare/cssnano@9.5.0...cssnano@9.6.0)

#### What's Changed

##### New Features

- feat(postcss-merge-longhand): merge `row-gap`/`column-gap`, `overscroll-behavior-x`/`-y`, `flex-direction`/`flex-wrap`, `column-rule-*` and equal `overflow-x`/`-y` into their shorthands when every target supports them

##### Bug Fixes

- refactor: remove cssesc dependency

- fix: improve escape handling in selectors

- fix: update postcss-calc

- fix: accept the optional leading rounding strategy of `round()`, such as `round(up, 1s, 1s)`, when postcss-ordered-values orders animations and transitions and when postcss-merge-longhand folds shorthands. The rounding strategy must be separated by a comma, so `round(up 1px, 2px)` is no longer treated as valid.

**Full Changelog**: <https://github.com/cssnano/cssnano/compare/cssnano@9.5.0...cssnano@9.6.0>
