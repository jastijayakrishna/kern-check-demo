# feat(playground): loading badge for the S-111 mosaic region swap (#1272 E-④)

## What & why

Fast slice of the mosaic-perf follow-up. Answers the original complaint that a ~10 MB region swap gave no feedback while it streamed in ("로드가 엄청 느려요... 로딩 표시가 없어 안 뜨는 것처럼 느껴짐"). A top-right badge appears the moment a swap starts a real network fetch and disappears on success; a failed swap flashes a brief message instead of hanging silently.

Chose this over the other two mosaic-perf items (multi-region simultaneous fill — needs an engine renderer-multiplexing change; nearest-region prefetch — needs adjacency logic) as the fast, purely-additive, zero-risk slice: no changes to existing fetch/cache/epoch behaviour, just two new optional callbacks + a DOM badge.

## How it works

- `playground/src/examples/s111-mosaic.ts`: two new optional `S111MosaicOptions` callbacks — `onLoadStart(modelKey)` fires only on a cache MISS (never on an LRU hit — that's instant, nothing to indicate), right before the `fetch`; `onLoadError(modelKey, err)` fires on a failed fetch/decode, epoch-guarded the same way the existing `current = prev` revert already is. Both purely additive — `onSwap`/`current()`/`setTime()` behaviour is unchanged.
- `playground/src/demo-runner.ts`: the badge (spinner + label) at top-right — top-left is the permanent hash-badge, bottom-centre is the already-landed time scrubber, so no overlap — wired into `setupCurrentsMosaic`/`teardownCurrentsMosaic`.
